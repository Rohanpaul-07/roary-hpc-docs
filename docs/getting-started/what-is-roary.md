## What is HPC?

<div class="hpc-definition-panel">

  <div class="hpc-definition-mark">
    HPC
  </div>

  <div>

    <span class="roary-page-eyebrow">
      HIGH PERFORMANCE COMPUTING
    </span>

    <h3>
      Computing at a scale that a normal workstation cannot provide
    </h3>

    <p>
      High Performance Computing uses powerful shared computing systems to
      solve problems that may be too large, too slow, or too resource-intensive
      for a typical desktop or laptop.
    </p>

      IMPORTANT

    <h3>
      Computational workloads belong on compute nodes
    </h3>

    <p>
      Running large calculations directly on a login node can consume resources
      shared by many users and affect the overall system.
    </p>

    <p>
      Submit computational workloads through Slurm so that the appropriate
      CPU, memory, and GPU resources are allocated to your job.
