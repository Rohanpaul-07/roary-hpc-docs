## Research Group and HPC Account Access

Roary uses research-group and project accounts to organize computational
resources.

A user may therefore need to be associated with the appropriate faculty,
laboratory, course, or research account in addition to having an individual
HPC login.

## What Happens After the Request?

The HPC Admins provision the account and the required cluster-side
associations.

Behind the scenes, Roary access may include:

<div class="provision-grid">

  <div class="provision-card">
    <span>USER</span>
    <strong>HPC Login</strong>
    <p>Your individual Roary user identity.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="provision-card">
    <span>GROUP</span>
    <strong>Research Association</strong>
    <p>Membership in the appropriate laboratory or project group.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="provision-card">
    <span>SLURM</span>
    <strong>Scheduler Account</strong>
    <p>Authorization to submit jobs under the appropriate HPC account.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="provision-card">
    <span>HOME</span>
    <strong>Home Environment</strong>
    <p>Your user environment and applicable filesystem access.</p>
  </div>

</div>

!!! info

    Users do not need to configure Slurm associations or operating-system
    groups themselves. These are administrative provisioning tasks handled
    by the HPC Admins.


## Access Is Not the Same as Every Resource

Your Roary account provides access according to the permissions and
allocations associated with your user.

For example, additional authorization may be required for:

- A specific faculty or research-group allocation
- Private queues or QoS resources
- Research-group storage
- Licensed scientific software
- Specialized computing resources

If a resource is not available after your account is active, please email
the HPC Admins at **hpcadmin@fiu.edu** for assistance.
